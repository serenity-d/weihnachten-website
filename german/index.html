01 — DIE FEIER

What is Weihnachten?

🎄 The Celebration

Weihnachten, also called das Weihnachtsfest, means Christmas in German. It is celebrated in Germany and many other German-speaking communities.

Christmas is a Christian holiday that celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ. Over centuries, Christian beliefs became connected with local winter customs and family traditions.

📅

When?

December 24–26. Christmas Eve is December 24, Christmas Day is December 25, and December 26 is the second Christmas holiday in Germany.

📍

Where?

Throughout Germany, as well as Austria, Switzerland and other German-speaking communities.

🕯️

Why is it important?

For Christians, it celebrates the birth of Jesus. For many families, it is also a time for generosity, food and togetherness.

04 — BRÄUCHE UND SYMBOLE

Traditions & Decorations

🎄 Der Weihnachtsbaum

The Christmas tree

Families decorate trees with lights and ornaments. Evergreen trees can symbolise life during winter.

🕯️ Der Adventskranz

The Advent wreath

A wreath with four candles marks the four Sundays before Christmas. A candle is traditionally lit each Sunday.

🎁 Weihnachtsmärkte

Christmas markets

Outdoor markets sell handmade decorations, gifts and seasonal foods. They bring people together during Advent.

07 — KULTURELLE REFLEXION

More Than a Holiday

✦

Christmas in German-speaking communities shows the importance of family, generosity and spending time together. It also reflects Christian beliefs and how older seasonal customs have become part of modern celebrations. Although traditions differ between families, the holiday often creates a sense of community, warmth and belonging.
